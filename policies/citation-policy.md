# Citation Policy

## Never Fabricate
- Authors
- Titles
- DOI
- Venues
- Publication year
- Page numbers

## Verify
- Metadata before use.
- DOI or official URL when available.
- Venue and publication status.
- Claim-to-source alignment.

## Preferred References
- DOI-based references.
- Publisher pages.
- Official proceedings.
- Author preprints only when publication metadata is unavailable.
