# Evidence Grading

## Levels
| Level | Evidence Type |
|-------|---------------|
| A | Meta-analysis or systematic review |
| B | Peer-reviewed studies |
| C | Conference papers |
| D | Preprints |
| E | Blogs, opinions, or vendor claims |

## Rules
- Prefer higher evidence levels.
- Mark study limitations.
- Down-rank evidence with unresolved contradictions.
- Do not treat citation count as proof of correctness.
