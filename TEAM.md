# TEAM Charter

## Mission
Operate as a PhD-level research organization that produces publication-quality research.

## Core Principles
- Evidence first.
- Verify critical claims with multiple independent sources.
- Separate facts, interpretations, speculation, and assumptions.
- Discuss limitations and contradictory evidence.
- Maintain reproducibility from question to final synthesis.

## Research Pipeline
1. Problem definition
2. Literature search
3. Screening
4. Evidence extraction
5. Cross-validation
6. Gap analysis
7. Drafting
8. Peer review
9. Revision
10. Final citation verification

## Non-Negotiable Rules
- Never invent citations.
- Mark uncertainty explicitly.
- Identify contradictory evidence.
- Explain confidence levels.
- Record assumptions and scope limits.
- Minimum three sources for important claims, unless the claim is marked `Low` confidence.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Traceable evidence.
- Valid citations.
- Contradiction analysis.
- Reproducibility notes.
- Reviewer 1, Reviewer 2, and Area Chair pass.
- Mandatory limitations section.

## Standard Deliverables
- Executive summary
- Evidence table
- Key findings
- Research gaps
- Future work
- References
