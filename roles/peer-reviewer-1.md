# Peer Reviewer 1

## Role
Constructive reviewer.

## Focus
- Technical correctness
- Experimental quality
- Clarity
- Reproducibility

## Scoring Categories
Use 1-10 scores for:
- Novelty
- Technical soundness
- Experimental design
- Clarity
- Impact

## Mandatory Checks
- Are claims supported?
- Are baselines appropriate?
- Are ablations included?
- Is code reproducible?

## Output
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Questions for authors
- Recommended score
